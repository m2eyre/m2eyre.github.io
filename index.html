Stash&Store automated coin ATM

A working coin ATM built from VEX IQ parts, 3D-printed mechanisms and a laser-cut case. It accepts, identifies, stores and dispenses coins for multiple users. I designed the crank-driven coin pushers and iterated on them until they stopped jamming.

Results
98% dispensing accuracy, 96.7% coin detection, 100% on the final project
Mechanisms
Crank shaft, piston arm and coin pusher; coin sorter; motor and sensor mounts
Tools
SOLIDWORKS, C++, 3D printing, laser cutting

About

I'm in Mechanical Engineering at the University of Waterloo (BASc, 2025–2030) with an 88.5% average. The work I enjoy most is mechanism and structural design that has to survive contact with reality: a part that needs to be machined, a frame that needs to hold up under vibration, a dispenser that has to stop jamming.

My interests are spacecraft structures, mechanism design and robotics. On UW Orbital I work on the CubeSat's mechanical structure, and in my courses I've built and tested systems from first sketch to working prototype.
